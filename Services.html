03 — Common Questions

Things people ask

How long does a typical project take? +
A single landing page can be done in about a week. A full multi-page site usually takes 2–4 weeks. Java web applications vary more — anywhere from 3–8 weeks depending on complexity. I'll give you a realistic timeline in the quote, not an optimistic one.
Do you work with clients outside South Africa? +
Yes — most of my communication is by email or video call, so location isn't a barrier. I'm in SAST (UTC+2) and I keep flexible hours for overlap with European and American time zones.
What do you need from me to get started? +
A rough idea of what you want, any existing designs or content you have, and a sense of your timeline and budget. If you don't have everything figured out yet, that's fine — we can work through it together.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance? +
Yes. I offer ongoing support arrangements for sites and apps I've built, and I'm also happy to take over maintenance for existing projects (after a quick review of the codebase). We can agree on a monthly retainer or an hourly rate.
Can you work with my existing website or codebase? +
Usually, yes. I'll take a look at what you have and give you an honest assessment — sometimes it's better to fix what's there, sometimes it's better to rebuild. I'll tell you which and why.
What's your payment structure? +
For most projects, it's 50% upfront and 50% on completion. For larger projects, we can split it into milestones. Hourly work is billed weekly. All details are agreed in writing before any work begins.
